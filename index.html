About

Subsurface geoscientist and petroleum engineer

MSc in Petroleum Geoscience from the University of Manchester and a BTech in Petroleum Engineering (First Class with Distinction). I combine seismic interpretation, petroleum systems analysis, basin modelling, source-rock geochemistry, petrophysics and prospect evaluation with reservoir engineering.

MSc Petroleum Geoscience

Developed practical experience in seismic interpretation, structural geology, sedimentology, stratigraphy, petrophysics, formation evaluation, source-rock geochemistry, basin analysis, 1D basin modelling, petroleum systems analysis, prospect evaluation and petroleum economics.

Dissertation: Post-Miocene Prospectivity in the Greater Alba Area: 1D Basin Modelling and Charge Evaluation

  • Interpreted 3D seismic in SLB Petrel: well-to-seismic ties, horizon and fault interpretation, velocity modelling, depth conversion and isopach mapping
  • Built 1D basin models in Sirius Novva™ covering burial history, heat flow calibration, source-rock maturity and hydrocarbon generation, comparing kinetic models to define uncertainty
  • Integrated well logs, petrophysics and geochemical data (TOC, vitrinite reflectance) into maturity and kitchen maps to evaluate charge and prospectivity
Petroleum engineering foundation

My BTech in Petroleum Engineering covered the full field life cycle: drilling engineering, reservoir engineering, reservoir fluid properties, well logging, formation evaluation, production engineering, well completion, well stimulation, enhanced oil recovery and hydrocarbon processing.

Industry experience · Tamilnadu Petroproducts Limited · 1 year
  • Worked on Normal Paraffin production (UOP technology): DCS operations, process monitoring, and equipment including distillation columns, heat exchangers, reactors, pumps and heaters
  • Interpreted P&IDs and PFDs and maintained MIS records
  • Supported site safety by issuing hot work, cold work and vessel entry permits, and by taking part in regular fire drills
Energy transition

Certificate in Carbon Capture and Storage (University of Edinburgh). My subsurface skills transfer directly to CO₂ storage and geothermal projects.

Leadership

As Program Chairperson of the SPE Student Chapter at Anna University (AUCT), I led webinars, industrial visits and field trips.

What I'm looking for

Graduate and entry-level roles as a Petroleum Geoscientist, Geologist, Geophysicist, Petroleum Engineer or Subsurface Analyst across oil and gas, CCS and geothermal. I'm open to relocation.
